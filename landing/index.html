    Do I need to know coding?
    No, and you never open a terminal. In the Claude app you upload one file. In Claude's desktop app, Codex or the ChatGPT app, you send one message and it sets everything up, asking before it changes anything. Some agents use small helper scripts that need Python, which is free; your AI checks for it and walks you through the one download if it's missing. Then you talk to the agents in plain English. Your first message is as simple as Run company-brain. My website is yourbrand.com.
    
      
    Does it work on free ChatGPT?
    Partly. The full version runs in Codex inside the ChatGPT desktop app, which comes with ChatGPT Plus and up (on Free it is still rolling out). In ChatGPT on the web, agents run as custom GPTs or uploaded skills, which can't run on a schedule or pull live ad data on their own. For daily autopilot use Claude, Codex, OpenClaw or Hermes. One download covers all of them.
    
      
    Is it monthly?
    No. You pay once. Crew includes 12 months of updates and new agents. Crew Pro and Done With You get lifetime updates. There is no monthly plan, ever.
    
      
    What extra costs are there?
    Reading your own ad, analytics, store and email data is free on every platform it uses. Image, video and data agents use your own keys, paid straight to the provider. Most runs cost ₹0 to a few hundred rupees; video costs more. Every agent shows an estimate first and waits for your yes.
    
      
    How do I get it after paying?
    Instantly. You get a download link on email and WhatsApp right after payment, plus the START HERE guide for your first 15 minutes.
    
      
    Is this just a pack of prompts?
    No. Each agent is a folder: a step-by-step playbook, settings you can change, tested scripts, templates, a worked example and a setup guide. It reads your live data, follows written rules, checks its own work, and asks before it spends, posts or sends.
    
      
    Will it work for my business?
    It's built for D2C brands, agencies, SaaS, creators and local businesses. The start guide tells you which 3 agents to run first for your type of business, so you see value in week one.
    
      
    Can I use it for clients?
    Yes, on Crew Pro and Done With You: up to 10 client brands, each with its own Company Brain. Crew is for your own business.
